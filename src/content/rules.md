The short version of what RPL is lives on [About](/about). This page is the
reference: the exact numbers, the procedures, and the things that are
deliberately still open.

**Last updated:** 9 September 2026 · **Applies to:** Season 2

## How these rules change

RPL is in its second season and isn't pretending to be a finished product.
These rules are a working set, refined season over season as real play
reveals what works, and adjusted for the fact that a league is made of
people rather than clauses. Some things below are explicitly undecided —
they're marked as such rather than filled in with a rule nobody has needed
yet.

When something does change, it changes between seasons wherever possible,
and the change is written into the log at the bottom of this page.

## Season 2

### The competition

- **Six clubs, three players each.** Eighteen rostered players.
- **Double round robin** — every club plays every other club twice, which is
  **30 series** across the season.
- **Regular-season series are best-of-three.**
- **A match day is two series**, with the remaining two clubs on a bye. The
  season is therefore **15 match days**.

### The schedule is a pool, not a calendar

There is no fixed fixture list. The season is a set of 15 lineups — each one
a pairing of two series plus two clubs on bye — and **any lineup can be
played on any night**. Whichever gets played next simply becomes the next
match day, numbered in the order actually played.

This is deliberate. Getting twelve people online at once is the real
constraint on a season, so the format bends to availability rather than the
other way round. What's left to play is on the [schedule](/schedule).

### Standings

- Clubs are ranked by **games won, not series won** — a 2–1 series win
  counts as two wins and a loss, not one win.
- Goal difference is shown alongside, and is used to order the table for
  display. It is **not** a ruling.
- **The tiebreaker for a genuine tie is not settled yet.** It will be decided
  if and when the league actually reaches one, rather than guessed at in
  advance.

### Playoffs

- **All six clubs qualify**, seeded by regular-season record.
- **Seeds 1 and 2 get a bye** straight to the semifinals.
- **Quarterfinals:** #3 vs #6, #4 vs #5.
- Quarterfinal winners join seeds 1 and 2 in the semifinals; the two
  semifinal winners meet in the final.
- **Every playoff series is best-of-seven** — quarterfinal, semifinal and
  final alike.

### The auction draft

Rosters are rebuilt from scratch every season.

- **Salary cap: $23,743 per club.** That's 110% of the previous season's
  championship roster value ($21,585), so the cap moves with how the league
  actually played rather than being set by hand.
- **Six captains are seeded onto their own clubs** at their own Player
  Value, which counts against that club's cap.
- **Nomination order** runs from the lowest-valued captain to the
  highest-valued, repeating each round until every roster is full.
- **Any captain may bid**, including the one who nominated.
- **Roughly a three-minute window per player.** No minimum raise. The
  highest bid when time expires wins, and comes off that club's cap.
- **Rosters lock when the draft ends.** No trades and no roster changes for
  the rest of the season.

What each club paid, and what those players are worth now, is on the
[draft report card](/draft).

### Player Value

Every player carries one number, and it's the currency the draft runs on.

- It starts from the player's **previous-season performance**, and moves
  during the season based on goals, assists, saves, shots and points,
  compared against other active players at the same in-game rank.
- A player **new to RPL** has no prior season to carry forward, so their
  starting value is **seeded from the average for their in-game rank**.
  Those players are marked as seeded wherever their prior value is shown.
- Values **update every match day**, and the blend leans less on a player's
  prior value the more games they play.
- **Exempt status:** a player far outside the league's usual skill range
  distorts everyone else's value, because the model scales players against
  each other rather than against an absolute standard. Such a player is
  marked Exempt — excluded from the calculations that set everyone else's
  value, with their own value set by hand instead. Their stats are still
  tracked and still count for awards.

Current values are on the [leaderboard](/leaderboard), and the model is
explained in more detail there.

### Match play

- **Substitutes:** a sub's stats are not credited to either team in the
  tracker. The result stands; the individual numbers don't count.
- **No-shows are not covered by a written rule yet.** Whether a series gets
  played short, postponed or forfeited is decided by the admins at the time.
  If it starts happening often enough to need a standing answer, it'll get
  one and be logged here.
- **A player leaving mid-season** is likewise unwritten. Roster lock means
  there's no replacement mechanism, so this would be handled case by case.

### Season awards

Four races, tracked across the regular season for **active rostered players
only**:

- **Season MVP** — highest average points per game.
- **Golden Boot** — most goals.
- **Golden Laces** — most assists.
- **Golden Gloves** — highest average saves per game.

Where each race currently stands is on [Currently](/currently).

### Conduct

Be respectful. No cheating and no harassment. Admins have the final say.

That's deliberately general rather than an itemised list of violations —
this is a league of people who know each other, and the specific rule that
matters is the one about admins having the final say.

## Change log

**Season 2** (current)

- Grew from **five clubs to six**; 18 players drafted instead of 15.
- Replaced the **snake draft with a live auction** and a salary cap, so a
  roster is a set of bets against a budget rather than a fixed pick order.
- Introduced the **salary cap**, set at 110% of the previous champion's
  roster value.
- Introduced **Exempt status** for players outside the model's usual range.

**Season 1**

- Five clubs of three. Snake draft. Best-of-three series. The Player Value
  model in its first form, with no prior-season values to carry forward.
- One club withdrew partway through and the schedule was rebalanced around
  the four that remained.
- The full record — champion, award winners and the closing value board — is
  in the [Season 1 archive](/seasons/1).
