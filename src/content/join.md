## What RPL is

The Rocket Premier League is a drafted, season-long Rocket League league. Six clubs of three
players each, run by manager-captains who build their rosters in a live auction. Everyone plays a
double round robin, every result is tracked, and every team makes the playoffs.

It is competitive, and it is also a group of people who mostly know each other. Both of those are
on purpose.

## What it actually costs you

This is the part most leagues are vague about, so here it is plainly.

- **One series per match night.** Best-of-three. That's roughly 30 to 45 minutes of actual play.
- **About ten match nights across a season.** The schedule runs fifteen match days, with two
  clubs on bye each night, so your club plays ten of them.
- **Pace is about one to two series a week.** Match nights are scheduled around the people
  playing, not fixed to a calendar.
- **No practice requirement.** No scrims, no VOD review, no attendance policy. Show up on your
  match nights.

If you can find half an hour, once or twice a week, you can play a full RPL season.

## You do not need to be good

The league spans a wide range of skill, deliberately. Every player carries a **Player Value** — a
number built from how they've actually performed, seeded from rank if they're new. Managers bid
against a salary cap, so a cheaper player isn't a worse pick, they're a different kind of pick.
Whole draft strategies are built on finding value low in the pool.

Practically: you get drafted at your level rather than measured against everyone else's. Being the
cheapest player in the auction is a role, not a rejection. It has happened, and that player got a
starting spot.

New to RPL entirely? That's fine — a third of the current league had never played a season before
this one.

## How the draft works

1. **You enter the draft pool.** Everyone in the pool gets a starting Player Value: carried
   forward from your previous RPL season if you have one, or seeded from your in-game rank if
   you're new.
2. **Six captains build the clubs.** Captains are assigned first and count against their own
   club's salary cap at their own value.
3. **The rest of the pool goes to auction.** Captains take turns nominating players, everyone can
   bid, roughly three minutes per player, highest bid wins. The money comes off that club's cap.
4. **Rosters lock.** No trades, no roster changes for the rest of the season.

Draft night is the best night of the season. Bidding wars, cap panic, and at least one steal that
somebody complains about for weeks.

## What Player Value means

Player Value is the league's single number for a player, and it's what the whole economy runs on.
It moves with how you perform across a season — goals, assists, saves, and the rest — so your
value next season is a product of how you actually played this one.

It is not a ranking of who is best. It's a price. Rising value means your performance is
outpacing what you cost, and the league notices.

You can see everyone's current value on the [leaderboard](/leaderboard).

## What's expected of you

Be respectful, don't cheat, don't harass anyone, and show up for the nights your club is playing.
Admins have the final say on anything that isn't covered. That's the whole rulebook on conduct,
and it has been enough so far.

The one thing that genuinely matters: your club is three people. If you disappear, two other
people can't play.

## Getting in

Everything runs through the RPL Discord — schedule, results, draft night, and the arguments in
between.

**[Join the Discord](https://discord.gg/a7K6xWQ9dE)**, then say you want in on the next draft.
An admin will get you added to the pool and you'll be pinged when draft night is scheduled. You
don't need a team, a rank, or anyone to vouch for you — the draft handles all of that.

Want to look around first? The [standings](/standings), [clubs](/teams) and
[player pages](/players) are all public. Start there and see whether it looks like your kind of
thing.
